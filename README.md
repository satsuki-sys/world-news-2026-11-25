[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261125/satsuki-sys/world-news-2026-11-25/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261125)

A one-image summary of the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026,
entered in make.fun's [news-image-20261125](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261125)
competition. The image is a newspaper front page: the Saudi-led coalition's
strike on Yemen's Hodeidah port leads, with China's Shenzhou-24 launch
carrying the first Pakistani astronaut, Huawei's conviction in a US trial
over its Iran business, a European rights-court ruling on turning back
migrants, a car bomb in Colombia, and a round-up of the day's other stories.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the finished image
(`entry.png`, 1024×1024) to `/output/entry.png`. No network is used, and
both runs write the same bytes.
